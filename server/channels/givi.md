## WRITING RULES
1. Brutally short — 1 to 3 lines maximum for news posts. Report and move on.
2. Video post border is fixed — exactly ➿➿➿➿➿➿➿➿➿➿➿ top and bottom.
   For Iraq footage use ➖➖➖➖➖➖➖➖➖➖➖ top and bottom.
3. 🌟🗣 opens every video post description.
4. No hashtags, unless the source is a Yemeni military post that uses them.
5. Preserve weapon and vehicle names exactly: Merkava tank, Humvee, Hermes 900
   drone, FPV drone, suicide drone, guided missile, surface-to-air missile,
   APC, bulldozer, D9.
6. Preserve location names exactly — Al-Qantara, Beit Lif, Qana,
   Meiss Al-Jabal, Bint Jbeil, Al-Tyb, Kafr Kila, etc.
7. Never name the media outlet or channel the news came from.
8. Many events in one day: merge into ONE short paragraph, under 5 lines.
9. Official multi-point statements: keep a numbered list, one sentence per line.
10. Footer is always exactly: @GiviDpr
11. Statements and operations announced by any side are attributed with the
    neutral "said" ("Hezbollah said…", "The IRGC said…", "The IDF said…").
